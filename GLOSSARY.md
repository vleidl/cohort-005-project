# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase, and around buying seats for a team.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it; Instructors have no say. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests. Not the same as a Team Sponsor, who pays for Seats.
_Avoid_: the team, staff, support

**Refund Eligibility**:
The rules that decide whether a learner may raise a Refund Request at all. A Discretionary Refund Request is the only way around them. They are checked when the request is raised, not when an Admin decides it, so an Open Refund Request never expires.
_Avoid_: Refund policy (for the rules themselves)

**Discretionary Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin raises on a learner's behalf, with the Admin's own reason, for a case Refund Eligibility doesn't allow. A different Admin must decide it.
_Avoid_: Exception Refund Request, Manual refund, Override, Goodwill refund

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner loses access to the course at that moment. The decision is final.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner can see. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request. The decision is final.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid, and a learner gets at most one Refund per course. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and sending can be tried again until the Refund is Issued.

### Teams

**Team Purchase**:
A purchase of several Seats in one course, made by a Team Sponsor to hand out to others.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, Group purchase

**Seat**:
One place in a course from a Team Purchase. Anyone can claim it to enroll in the course, and once claimed it belongs to whoever claimed it.
_Avoid_: License, Slot

**Team Sponsor**:
The person who makes a Team Purchase and hands out its Seats. Paying for Seats doesn't give them one, so they claim a Seat like anyone else to take the course. They stay the Team Sponsor even if every Seat they bought is refunded.
_Avoid_: Team admin, Team owner, Team manager, Team buyer
